Aurora Audio

Kybaska, Aadttrai, Samgol

See Your Music

Aurora Audio is an Android app that turns any sound into a living visualizer. Play a song from your phone, or capture audio from any other app on your device, and watch the music come alive as a 24-dimensional rotating lattice and a real time spectrum analyzer. Tilt the phone to push the lattice through space, customize colors and motion to taste, and let the visuals run full screen.

Features

  • FFT Spectrum Bars. Real time audio bars driven by an on-device FFT. Choose anywhere from 24 to 768 bars, set rise speed, fall speed, sensitivity, max height, and opacity.
  • The Lattice. A 24 dimensional rotating geometric structure projected onto the screen. Each dimension breathes with a different frequency band, so the geometry expands and contracts to the music.
  • Gyroscope Reactive. Tilt your phone to rotate the lattice in space. Each axis can be inverted in settings, and the lattice auto recenters when the device is held still.
  • Two Audio Sources. Pick local audio files for direct playback with album art and a queue, or use system wide screen capture to visualize audio from any other app on your phone.
  • Audio Synced Visuals. The visualizer reads the audio sink playback position so the visuals stay in time with what you are hearing on every device, regardless of buffer latency.
  • Color Modes. Default animated palette, a solid color you pick yourself, or a full rainbow cycle. Available for both the bars and the lattice.
  • Tabbed Settings. Lattice, Bars, Gyro, and General tabs. Sliders show a marker at the default value and snap to it on release, so you never lose your starting point. One tap reset button restores everything.
  • Full Screen Visuals. Tap once to bring up controls, then they fade out so the visuals own the screen.
